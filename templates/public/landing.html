How it works

Posted. Built. Reviewed. Signed.

  1. 1

    A company posts the brief

    Real work with a real deadline, not an exercise. We verify the company before anything of theirs goes live.

  2. 2

    Students take it on

    A lecturer places their class, or a student anywhere applies on their own. Teams form and apply as one.

  3. 3

    The work is reviewed

    Lecturer, then university, then the company. Anything that is not ready comes back with a reason, not a rejection.

  4. 4

    It is signed and recorded

    Four signatures on a record an employer can check — the student, the supervisor, the institution and the client.

Feedback the student cannot write

A certificate proves the work happened. This says how it went — and it comes from the account that posted the brief and approved the delivery.

  • Written by the client, not the candidate. Only the company that commissioned the brief can leave it, from the account that signed the work off.
  • Only on finished work. The form does not exist until the deliverable has been through the lecturer, the institution and the company.
  • Four competencies, not one star. Communication, problem solving, quality and professionalism, so a reader learns what was good rather than how good.
  • The student chooses what is public — and cannot edit a word of it. Hiding a review is theirs to do. Changing one is not.
