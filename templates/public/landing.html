How it works

Posted. Built. Reviewed. Signed.

  1. 1

    A company posts the brief

    Real work with a real deadline, not an exercise. We verify the company before anything of theirs goes live.

  2. 2

    Students take it on

    A lecturer places their class, or a student anywhere applies on their own. Teams form and apply as one.

  3. 3

    The work is reviewed

    Lecturer, then university, then the company. Anything that is not ready comes back with a reason, not a rejection.

  4. 4

    It is signed and recorded

    Four signatures on a record an employer can check — the student, the supervisor, the institution and the client.
