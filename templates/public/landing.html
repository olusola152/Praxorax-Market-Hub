How the work flows

01

Company posts a brief

A real task with a deadline, required skills and a field of study. No fee to students, no bidding.

02

Lecturer takes it on

Any open brief. The lecturer assigns it to their own students as practical coursework.

03

Students build it

Four supervised phases against the deadline, with the lecturer tracking progress throughout.

04

Work is reviewed

Student to lecturer to university to company. Anyone in the chain can send it back for correction.

05

Company approves

A certificate is issued and the project lands in the student's verified portfolio.
